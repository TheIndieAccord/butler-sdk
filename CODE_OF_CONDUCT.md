# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The main points: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may
remove comments, close issues or block people who do not follow it.

To report a problem, email **dev@theindieaccord.com**. Reports are kept private.
